# Example: Solitude with Strength

> Demonstration only. The scene is illustrative, not a verified analysis of a specific viral image.

## Creative brief
- Task: create a photorealistic fashion editorial image.
- Intended use: fashion creator portfolio / social post.
- Audience: viewers interested in refined, contemporary fashion imagery.
- Primary idea: a composed woman remains emotionally self-possessed within a vast, impersonal space.
- Primary emotion: solitude with strength.
- Overall tone: restrained, cool, quietly confident.
- Visual hook: a small, stable human figure against monumental architecture and open sea.

## Visual recipe
- Core relationship: human scale versus architectural/environmental scale.
- Pose: stable weight, relaxed shoulders, gaze toward the horizon, no exaggerated sadness.
- Wardrobe: a flowing black dress; fabric movement provides a soft counterpoint to rigid architecture.
- Composition: environmental space remains important; subject slightly off-center, but still large enough to read the silhouette.
- Light: soft overcast daylight with readable skin and fabric detail.
- Color: restrained blue-grey environment and deep neutral clothing; avoid muddy shadows.
- Flexible variables: location, architectural style, dress details, hairstyle, weather intensity.
- Must preserve: stable body language, environmental scale contrast, clear focal hierarchy.
- Avoid: generic centered catalog pose, excessive fog, underexposure, melodramatic expression, random accessories.

## Main prompt
Photorealistic luxury fashion editorial featuring a fictional adult East Asian woman wearing an elegant flowing black dress, standing beside monumental modern seaside architecture. Wide composition with generous negative space and a clear scale contrast between the woman and the environment. She looks quietly toward the distant horizon, shoulders relaxed and posture grounded, conveying solitude with inner strength rather than sadness. Soft overcast daylight, readable shadow detail, muted blue-grey sea, realistic skin texture, believable fabric movement in a gentle coastal breeze, refined editorial art direction, natural anatomy and subtle depth.

## Avoid
Overly dark exposure, muddy grey haze, exaggerated sadness, theatrical pose, plastic skin, overcomplicated accessories, generic centered catalog composition.

## Quality checks
1. Does the image communicate composed solitude rather than helplessness?
2. Is the figure small enough to establish scale but large enough for the outfit to read?
3. Is there one clear focal hierarchy?
4. Do fabric movement and environmental conditions feel physically plausible?
5. Does the result have a memorable relationship beyond merely using a black dress and a grey-blue background?
